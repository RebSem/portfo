## What it is

[cursivo.xyz](https://cursivo.xyz) is a CRM with an AI layer for car rental companies. It is built for operators whose deals and fleet economics live in Excel and WhatsApp, and whose decisions are made by gut feel. It is my own product.

<details>
<summary>The problem and the decision</summary>

### The problem

Car rental is an operationally heavy business: leads, documents, client screening, maintenance, downtime, seasonality. Without an end-to-end system the owner can't see:

- where leads actually drop off and which managers lose conversion;
- which clients carry anomalous risk (documents, history, behavior);
- which car is already running at a loss once downtime and service are factored in.

All three usually get patched by hand or not at all.

### The decision

Put all three into one workflow and have the AI suggest actions instead of producing reports. The bet behind it: in rental the winner isn't the operator with the most cars, it's the one who makes the right call fastest. Cursivo turns scattered data into a management decision.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How the product works</summary>

- **End-to-end sales funnel.** Time-to-reply, stage conversion and bottlenecks, without a separate BI tool or spreadsheet.
- **AI client and document scoring.** Incoming data gets structured, and risk is flagged before the car leaves the lot.
- **Fleet unit economics.** Per-car profitability, accounting for downtime, seasonality and operating costs.
- **AI suggestions for action, not reports.** Concrete recommendations on pricing, retiring a model, or rebalancing manager workload.

</details>
