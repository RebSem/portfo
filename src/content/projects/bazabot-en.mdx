## What it is

BazaBot is an internal Telegram bot for marketers and sales managers. You send it a `.csv` or `.xlsx` contact list, it finds the phone column, cleans the numbers and returns a base ready for an SMS or calling campaign. I did both the product side and the build.

<details>
<summary>The problem and the decision</summary>

Contact bases in Excel are almost always dirty: mixed prefixes (8, 7, +7, none), duplicates, landlines mixed with mobile, invalid rows. Without normalization the budget goes on reaching nobody, and manual cleanup takes a manager hours.

So the work moved out of spreadsheets and into Telegram: a surface chosen for the audience and the scenario, not for how it looks in a portfolio.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How the product works</summary>

- **In-chat file processing.** The user drops a `.csv` or `.xlsx`, the bot detects the phone column and offers cleanup options.
- **Normalization to international format.** Deduplication and fixes for common typos.
- **HLR check via p1sms.** The bot identifies the actual carrier and flags numbers that cannot receive SMS.

**Stack:** Python, Aiogram, Pandas/Openpyxl, p1sms API.

</details>
