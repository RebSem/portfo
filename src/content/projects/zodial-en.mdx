## Result

- **3 days** from idea to a working pipeline with real payments and delivery.
- **No human in the loop:** payment, computation, PDF and email run on their own.
- **About a minute** from birth data to a finished PDF.
- **20+ pages** of unique content per order.

## What it is

Personal PDF reports built from birth data. The customer enters date, time and place of birth, pays, and gets the report by email. A pet project to test whether such a funnel can run with nobody in it, in a real niche. I was the product owner and built it myself.

<details>
<summary>The problem and the decision</summary>

In personal reports (astrology, numerology, fortune-telling) most work is manual: customers pay, wait hours or days, and get PDFs in inconsistent layouts, sometimes with manual-entry errors. That hurts conversion and LTV.

The decision: take the human out completely. A pet project like this tells me in a weekend whether a model works, before a team spends weeks on it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How the product works</summary>

- **Form:** date, time and place of birth, with automatic coordinate lookup.
- **Payment:** a YooKassa webhook starts the computation once payment is confirmed.
- **PDF:** an HTML/CSS layout engine builds each report.
- **Delivery:** transactional email via Resend, PDF attached right after generation.

**Stack:** Next.js, Prisma + SQLite, YooKassa API, Puppeteer/React-pdf, Resend.

</details>
