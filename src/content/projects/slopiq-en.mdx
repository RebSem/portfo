## Result

- **7 days from idea to a working build** I can launch on my own iPhone on the slope.
- **Runs and lifts split automatically**: no manual mode switching.
- **AI assistant for the slope**: wax advice for the current weather, a daily summary, safety notes.
- **Hyperlocal weather**: Open-Meteo forecast by coordinates and altitude.

## What it is

Slopiq is a native iOS app for skiers and snowboarders: run tracking, weather and session history. The interface is made for gloves and cold: large controls, high contrast, minimal gestures. A pet project I built for myself to play with SwiftUI.

<details>
<summary>The problem and the decision</summary>

Existing trackers fall short in one of three ways:

- too much interface: small buttons don't mix with gloves and frost;
- nothing beyond raw speed numbers;
- no weather for a specific altitude.

Slopiq answers each one: a glove-friendly interface, an AI assistant on top of the numbers, and weather by coordinates and altitude.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Under the hood</summary>

SwiftUI, CoreLocation, Open-Meteo API, OpenAI / YandexGPT.

</details>
