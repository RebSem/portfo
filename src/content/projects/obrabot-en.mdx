## What it gave the team

- **Calls the team can review:** what the agents talk about, where the script breaks and which calls need a person to listen.
- **Prompt versions compared on data:** dashboards by agent and script, scenario A/B, daily and per-campaign trends.
- **Prompts and proposals in one place:** a registry of product prompts with a library of vetted references, and a generator for commercial proposals.
- **A working internal tool** for the group's main product line, in production; the numbers stay under NDA.

## What it is

Obrabot is the internal workspace of the voice AI team in the Prof-IT group, where voice AI is the main product line, not a pilot. Customers see their own call results in the Zvonobot AI console; obrabot is where our team prepared prompts and proposals and reviewed calls. I owned it as the Product Manager until October 2026: requirements, backlog priorities, and aligning UX and the data model with the team and engineers.

<details>
<summary>The problem and the decision</summary>

Without the right dashboard a voice AI agent is a black box. The team can't see:

- which segments of calls go off-script;
- where the AI agent performs worse than a human and why;
- which specific calls a person actually needs to listen to;
- how the conversation metric shifts from iteration to iteration.

Without this layer the team has to find weak spots call by call. So the workspace goes from campaign trends down to a single call with its transcript and audio.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is in the workspace</summary>

- **AI call segmentation.** Calls are tagged automatically by outcome, refusals, tone and unusual behavior.
- **Drill-down to a call.** Transcript, tags, audio and metadata on one screen.
- **Early signals.** Dialogs where a human should step in are flagged before the customer complains.
- **Prompt generation.** A registry of product prompts and a library of vetted references; a new voice prompt is assembled from the client's brief, spec and call transcript.
- **Commercial proposals.** A short form on the task, the niche and the integration; the proposal comes out with development cost and a per-second rate.

</details>
