<details>
<summary>The problem and the decision</summary>

Without the right dashboard a voice AI agent is a black box. The team can't see:

- which segments of calls go off-script;
- where the AI agent performs worse than a human and why;
- which specific calls a person actually needs to listen to;
- how the conversation metric shifts from iteration to iteration.

Without this layer the product sells worse: the customer doesn't trust what they can't verify. So the console goes from campaign trends down to a single call with its transcript and audio.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How the product works</summary>

- **AI call segmentation.** Calls are tagged automatically by outcome, refusals, tone and unusual behavior.
- **Drill-down to a call.** Transcript, tags, audio and metadata on one screen.
- **Early signals.** Dialogs where a human should step in are flagged before the customer complains.
- **Prompt generation.** A registry of product prompts and a library of vetted references; a new voice prompt is assembled from the client's brief, spec and call transcript.
- **Commercial proposals.** A short form on the task, the niche and the integration; the proposal comes out with development cost and a per-second rate.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Under the hood</summary>

Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma + PostgreSQL, OpenAI API, integrations with Prof-IT infrastructure.
